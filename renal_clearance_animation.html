Inulin — ideal GFR marker

Inulin is freely filtered, not reabsorbed and not secreted. Its clearance equals GFR.

C(inulin) = GFR

Illustrative model: filtered/excreted loads use relative units; glucose in diabetes is modelled with a threefold plasma concentration and a fixed reabsorptive capacity. In renal failure, creatinine excretion initially falls at unchanged plasma concentration; at a new steady state plasma creatinine rises and urinary excretion again approaches production. Inulin clearance continues to equal GFR.
